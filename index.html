    Students of the Ummah, learning with purpose

    SOU — Tullab al-Ummah — is an online learning community for students aged 16+ — in college, university or working — who want to study the Qur’an, Arabic and Islamic sciences properly: with qualified teachers, a clear structure and real care for every learner.

    Our story

    Why we started SOU

    Many students told us the same thing: it was hard to find classes that were both authentic and well organised. Lessons were cancelled, progress wasn’t tracked, and adults felt there was no place for beginners.

    SOU was founded to change that. We bring together experienced teachers, a structured curriculum and simple tools — so learners always know what they are studying, how they are progressing and what comes next.

    Tajweed for Beginners: a new evening cohort for adults

    HUstadha Hadjer · 08 Nov 2026 · 4 min read

    Many adults tell us the same thing: they can read a little, but they’re not confident they’re reciting correctly. Our new evening cohort is designed exactly for that — a calm, structured course that rebuilds your recitation from the foundations.

    What you’ll learn

    • Correct pronunciation of every letter (makharij)
    • The rules of noon sakinah, meem sakinah and madd
    • Applying the rules while reading short surahs
    • Building a daily practice you can keep
    “The goal isn’t to rush through rules — it’s to recite with confidence and love for the words of Allah.”

    How the course works

    The course runs for 10 weeks, one 45-minute live session per week, in small groups of up to six learners. Every session is recorded, and you can keep diary notes linked to each lesson video in your student portal.

    Short quick quizzes each week help you check your understanding, and your teacher gives personal feedback on your recitation.

    Who it’s for

    Adults aged 16+ who can recognise Arabic letters but want to read correctly. Not sure about your level? Book a free taster class first and your teacher will advise you.
